PROPERTY LAW · GUIDE

Preparing for a Property Transfer

A practical checklist before you sign or transfer.

Gather the ownership and transaction documents before instructing a conveyancer.

Ask about transfer costs, timelines, existing bonds and conditions in the agreement.

A conveyancer can help identify registration requirements for the particular transaction.

Understanding Workplace Disputes

Questions to ask early in an employment matter.

Keep key documents, notices and correspondence in chronological order.

Why a Current Will Matters

Clear planning today can reduce uncertainty later.
